# Research: Splitgate, Splitgate 2, "Portal Wars"

## Splitgate (1)
- Free-to-play FPS by 1047 Games; players create wormhole portals between two points and can shoot or travel through them.
- Originally named *Wormhole Wars*, renamed *Splitgate: Arena Warfare* in July 2018.
- Pitch is "Halo plus Portal"; bowl-shaped arenas. IGN: average arena FPS but the portal mechanic makes it smart and tactical. Metacritic 68.
- You can dispel an enemy portal without spending one of your own.

## Splitgate 2
- Portal gun on a tap; portals attach only to specific wall surfaces, so **map knowledge matters** and you cannot instantly teleport behind an enemy.
- "Auto triple portal" simplified opening and closing portals.
- Maps changed to three-lane layouts (more Call of Duty than Halo). Critics said map design did not maximise portal use.
- Dropped the ability to dispel enemy portals for free.
- Redesigned in 2025 (relaunch as *Splitgate: Arena Reloaded*): factions and abilities removed in favour of classic arena shooter format; portals remain a central pillar.
- Reception split: without portals it is "competent but uninspired"; portals don't add enough if maps don't force them.

## "Portal Wars"
No major multiplayer shooter of that name was found. A tiny itch.io jam game (lootss, Lowrez Jam 2021) is a portal defense game, unrelated. Treat "Portal Wars" as a name idea or as shorthand for the Splitgate-style concept. See [[08 Decisions]].

## Lessons for this project
1. Portals must be the reason maps are fun: design maps around portal surfaces first ([[04 Core Systems]]).
2. Keep the weapon set strong enough that the shooter works even with portals off (the main criticism).
3. Keep the ruleset classic: no classes, fixed weapon spawns.
4. Decide deliberately about portal counterplay (dispel, shoot-through, cooldowns).
