# Vision

## Pitch
Competitive multiplayer arena shooter on compact maps. Weapons spawn at fixed locations, movement is expressive and skill-based, matches end on a frag limit or a time limit. Think Quake / Unreal Tournament with **my own weapon set and map design**, plus a Portal-style **two-portal gun** (Q = left portal, E = right portal) as the signature mechanic.

## Pillars
1. **Movement is the skill.** Speed, air control, and portals are all part of one movement toolbox.
2. **Map control through fixed pickups.** Weapons and items spawn on timers at known locations; players route around them.
3. **Portals create angles, not chaos.** Maps are designed around portal surfaces so every portal has a counter.
4. **Short, readable matches.** Compact maps, frag limit or time limit, fast respawn.

## Non-goals (for now)
- Classes, abilities, battle pass (Splitgate 2 drifted here and reverted; see [[02 Research - Splitgate]]).
- Large maps or vehicles.
